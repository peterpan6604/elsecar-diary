Room hire terms

Elsecar Sports & Social Club, Armroyd Lane, Elsecar, S74 8ES
Function room and tea room

At a glance

  • £52 for any booking, either room, any session.
  • Pay after the committee approve your booking, using the card payment link in your confirmation email, within 7 days.
  • Function room up to 80 people. Tea room up to 50 people.
  • Cancel 29 days or more before: £42 back. 14 to 28 days before: £25 back. 13 days or fewer: no refund.

By sending a booking request you agree to these terms. "The Club" means Elsecar Sports & Social Club. "You" means the person who makes the booking. "The room" means the function room (also called the bar) or the tea room, whichever you book. These terms apply to both rooms.

1. Booking

1.1 You must be 18 or over to book.

1.2 Sending a request does not book the room. Your booking is confirmed only when a committee member approves it and we email you to say so.

1.3 You are responsible for the event and must be at the Club for the whole of it.

1.4 Tell us the real type of event and roughly how many guests when you book. If the event turns out to be different from what you told us, the Club may cancel the booking or end the event.

1.5 The Club may refuse any booking request without giving a reason.

1.6 The function room and the tea room are hired separately. Booking one room does not give you use of the other, and the other room may be hired by someone else at the same time.

2. Price and payment

2.1 Room hire costs £52 for every booking, for either room and for any session (afternoon, evening or whole day).

2.2 You do not pay anything when you send a request. When the committee approve your booking, the confirmation email includes a secure card payment link.

2.3 Please pay the £52 within 7 days of the confirmation email, or by the day of your event if that is sooner. The email gives the date. If the fee is not paid by then, the Club may cancel the booking and offer the date to someone else.

2.4 There is no separate deposit. The £52 is the full hire fee.

3. Changing your booking

3.1 If you want to move your booking to another date, ask us as soon as you can. A new date depends on the room being free.

3.2 You can move your booking to another free date once, at no charge, if you ask at least 14 days before the event.

3.3 Agree any change to the type of event or the number of guests with the Club before the event. Guest numbers can never go above the room's limit (section 6).

4. Cancelling your booking

4.1 To cancel, reply to any email we have sent you about your booking, or contact the Club. We count the date we receive your message as the date you cancelled.

4.2 How much we refund depends on how many days before your event you cancel. We count the days from the date you cancel to the date of the event. For example, for an event on Saturday 19 December, cancelling on Friday 20 November is 29 days before.

When you cancelRefund of your £52
More than 28 days before (29 days or more)£42 back: the full fee minus a £10 admin fee
From 28 days to 14 days before£25 back
Less than 14 days before (13 days or fewer)No refund

4.3 If you have not paid yet, nothing is due when you cancel.

4.4 Refunds go back to the card you paid with. They usually take 5 to 10 working days to arrive.

5. If the Club cancels

5.1 The Club may cancel a booking if it cannot safely or lawfully host the event, for example because of an emergency, a serious problem with the building, or a licensing issue. We will tell you as soon as we can.

5.2 If the Club cancels and it is not your fault, you get a full refund of the £52 if you have paid.

5.3 The Club is not responsible for other costs you have paid, such as entertainers, catering or decorations, as far as the law allows.

5.4 If the Club cancels because these terms have been broken (for example section 1.4 or section 12), the refund rules in section 4 apply instead.

6. Numbers

6.1 The function room holds a maximum of 80 people and the tea room a maximum of 50 people. This includes staff, DJs and entertainers. These are fire safety limits and can never be exceeded.

7. Times, setting up and clearing away

7.1 Sessions are: afternoon 12pm to 5pm, evening 6pm to midnight, or the whole day (12pm to midnight).

7.2 You can get into the room to decorate and set up from 1 hour before your session.

7.3 Music must finish by 11.30pm.

7.4 Guests must leave the building by midnight.

7.5 An afternoon booking must be cleared by 5pm, so the room is ready for the evening.

7.6 You are responsible for taking away everything you bring at the end of the event, including decorations, equipment, cakes, gifts and leftover food. Anything left behind may be thrown away.

8. Bar and alcohol

8.1 The Club is licensed. Alcohol bought elsewhere must not be brought onto the premises unless the committee agrees beforehand.

8.2 Alcohol can only be sold or served in line with the Club's licence. The bar manager has the final say on serving.

8.3 Bar staff follow Challenge 25. They will not serve anyone under 18, and may refuse to serve anyone, for example anyone who appears drunk.

8.4 Drinks must not be taken outside the building where the Club's licence or rules do not allow it.

9. Food and outside suppliers

9.1 You may bring your own buffet. Outside caterers need the committee's agreement first.

9.2 Bouncy castles, entertainers and other outside suppliers also need the committee's agreement first.

9.3 You are responsible for making sure your suppliers follow these terms and the Club's rules.

9.4 You are responsible for the food you provide, including telling your guests about allergens.

9.5 Tea room hirers can use the kitchen to prepare and serve food and drink they bring. Please leave it clean and tidy.

10. Decorations

10.1 Decorations must not damage walls, ceilings, paintwork or fixtures. Do not use nails, pins, staples or tape. Use free standing decorations, or ask the committee what is allowed.

10.2 Confetti, glitter, table sprinkles, candles, sparklers, smoke or haze machines and anything with a naked flame are not allowed unless the committee agrees beforehand. Smoke and haze can set off the fire alarm.

11. Music and entertainers

11.1 DJs, bands and entertainers must follow the Club's rules and licence conditions.

11.2 Any electrical equipment brought in must be safe and in good working order. The Club may ask to see proof that it has been safety (PAT) tested, and that entertainers have public liability insurance.

11.3 Keep music within any noise limits that apply to the Club. Club staff may ask for the volume to be turned down, to protect neighbours or a party in the other room, and you must agree to this.

12. Behaviour

12.1 You are responsible for making sure your guests behave reasonably.

12.2 Abusive, threatening or violent behaviour will not be tolerated. Illegal drugs are not allowed.

12.3 Club staff may ask any guest to leave, or end the event early, in serious cases. No refund is given if this happens.

12.4 Smoking and vaping are not allowed inside the building.

13. Children

13.1 Children must be supervised by a responsible adult at all times, inside and outside the building.

13.2 Club staff do not supervise children at private functions.

13.3 Under 18s must follow the Club's rules for children and young people.

14. Damage and cleaning

14.1 Leave the room in the condition you found it, apart from normal cleaning.

14.2 You must pay for any damage caused by you or your guests, as far as the law allows.

14.3 If the room is left in an unreasonable condition, the Club may charge for the extra cleaning.

14.4 There is no charge if a false fire alarm goes off during your event.

15. Safety

15.1 Keep fire exits and corridors clear at all times.

15.2 Never go over the room's limit (section 6).

15.3 A member of Club staff will show you the fire exits and assembly point on arrival. Please pass this on to your guests.

15.4 You and your guests must follow the instructions of Club staff in an emergency.

15.5 Report any accident or injury to Club staff straight away.

16. Leaving the Club

16.1 Please ask your guests to leave quietly and respect our neighbours.

16.2 Parking, taxis and collection: the Club car park is pay to park. Guests can enter their car registration on the iPad at the bar, pay for parking, or park as Club members. Please ask taxis to drop off and pick up at the clubhouse entrance.

17. Responsibility

17.1 The Club is not responsible for any loss of or damage to property brought onto the premises, including gifts, unless it was caused by the Club's own negligence.

17.2 Nothing in these terms limits the Club's responsibility for death or personal injury caused by its negligence.

18. Your details

18.1 The Club uses your name, email and phone number only to manage your booking. We do not share them with anyone else. Payments are handled by Stripe, who process your card details for us. The Club never sees your card number. We delete your contact details six months after your event.

Questions? Reply to any email we have sent you about your booking, or contact the Club.
