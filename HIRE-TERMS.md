# Function Room Hire Terms

**Elsecar Sports & Social Club, Armroyd Lane, Elsecar, S74 8ES**

By sending a booking request you agree to these terms. "The Club" means Elsecar Sports & Social Club. "You" means the person who makes the booking.

## 1. Booking

1.1 You must be 18 or over to book.

1.2 Sending a request does not book the room. Your booking is confirmed only when a committee member approves it and we email you to say so.

1.3 The person who books is responsible for the event and must be at the club for the whole event.

1.4 Tell us the real type of event when you book. If the event turns out to be different from what you told us, the Club may cancel the booking or end the event.

1.5 The Club may refuse any booking without giving a reason.

## 2. Prices and payment

2.1 Room hire prices are available from the committee **[confirm: until the price list is published]**.

2.2 There is no deposit. Any hire charge is paid **[confirm: on the day / before the event]**.

## 3. Changing or cancelling

3.1 If you need to cancel or change your booking, tell us as soon as you can, and at least **[confirm: 14] days** before the event, so the date can be offered to someone else.

3.2 The Club may cancel a booking if the room is needed for an emergency, essential repairs, or reasons outside its control. We will give as much notice as we can. The Club is not responsible for any costs you have already paid to other people (for example caterers or DJs).

## 4. Times

4.1 You can use the room from **[confirm: 1 hour]** before your start time to set up.

4.2 Music ends at **[confirm: 11:30pm]** and guests must leave by **[confirm: midnight]**, unless the committee agrees otherwise in writing.

4.3 Bar opening times follow the Club's licence. The bar manager has the final say on serving.

## 5. Numbers

5.1 The room holds a maximum of **75 people**, including staff, DJs and entertainers. This is a fire safety limit and cannot be exceeded.

## 6. Bar and alcohol

6.1 The Club is licensed. No alcohol may be brought onto the premises, except a celebration toast if the committee agrees beforehand **[confirm]**.

6.2 Bar staff follow Challenge 25 and will not serve anyone under 18, or anyone who appears drunk.

## 7. Food

7.1 You may bring your own buffet or use your own caterer **[confirm]**.

7.2 You are responsible for the food you provide, including telling your guests about allergens.

7.3 Take away or clear up all leftover food at the end of the event.

## 8. Decorations

8.1 Do not fix anything to walls, ceilings or paintwork with nails, pins, staples or tape. Use free standing decorations, or ask the committee what is allowed.

8.2 No confetti, glitter, table sprinkles, candles, sparklers or naked flames inside.

8.3 Smoke and haze machines can set off the fire alarm and are not allowed without committee permission.

8.4 Take down all decorations at the end of the event.

## 9. Music and entertainers

9.1 DJs, bands and entertainers must have their own public liability insurance, and their electrical equipment must be safety (PAT) tested. The Club may ask to see proof.

9.2 Keep music at a reasonable volume. Club staff may ask for the volume to be turned down to protect neighbours, and you must agree to this.

## 10. Children

10.1 Children must be supervised by an adult at all times, both inside and outside the building.

## 11. Behaviour

11.1 You are responsible for your guests' behaviour.

11.2 Club staff may end the event early, or ask any guest to leave, if there is violence, threatening behaviour, drug use, or a risk to safety. No refund is given in this case.

11.3 Smoking and vaping are not allowed inside the building.

## 12. Damage and cleaning

12.1 Leave the room in the condition you found it, apart from normal cleaning.

12.2 You must pay for any damage caused by you or your guests, and for any extra cleaning needed **[confirm: and any fire service call out charge caused by a false alarm]**.

## 13. Safety

13.1 Keep fire exits and corridors clear at all times.

13.2 A member of Club staff will show you the fire exits and assembly point on arrival. Please pass this on to your guests.

13.3 Report any accident or injury to Club staff straight away.

## 14. Responsibility

14.1 The Club is not responsible for any loss of or damage to property brought onto the premises, including gifts, unless it was caused by the Club's own negligence.

14.2 Nothing in these terms limits the Club's responsibility for death or personal injury caused by its negligence.

## 15. Your details

15.1 The Club uses your name, email and phone number only to manage your booking. We do not share them with anyone else. We delete them six months after your event.
