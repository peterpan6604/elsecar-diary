# Club Diary: Committee Guide

The Club Diary is where the committee and staff see what is on at the club and add or change things. It works on your phone. There is nothing to install.

## 1. Open it

Open the diary link the secretary sent you in your phone's browser (Safari on iPhone, Chrome on Android). Keep the link to the committee and staff only.

## 2. Put it on your phone's home screen

This gives you a Club Diary button, like an app.

**iPhone (Safari)**
1. Open the diary link in Safari.
2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow up).
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Tap **Add**.

**Android (Chrome)**
1. Open the diary link in Chrome.
2. Tap the three dots at the top right.
3. Tap **Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**).
4. Tap **Add**.

## 3. Sign in

1. Type your **first name**. It is only used to show who made each change.
2. Type the **diary code**. Everyone shares the same one. Ask the secretary if you do not have it.
3. Tap **Open diary**.

You stay signed in for 30 days. If the code is changed, everyone has to sign in again with the new one.

If you get the code wrong five times, the diary locks you out for 15 minutes. Wait, then try again.

## 4. Find your way around

* **Month** shows a calendar. Tap a day to see what is on it underneath. Coloured dots show there is something on that day.
* **List** shows everything coming up in the next three months, day by day.
* The coloured buttons (Club events, Cricket and so on) switch each type on or off.
* **Recent changes** at the bottom shows who changed what.
* **Sign out** is at the top.

## 5. Add an event

1. Tap **+ Add** (or **Add to this day**).
2. Type what is happening.
3. Choose the **type**, the **date**, and the **start** and **finish** times.
4. Add notes if you want. Only the committee sees notes.
5. Tick **Uses the function room** or **Uses the tea room** (or both) if it takes up that room. This stops the public booking that room for those times. The other room stays open to the public. For **Room hire** items, tick the room being hired.
6. Tick **Show on the public website** only if everyone should see it. Anything left unticked is for the committee only.
7. Tap **Add to diary**.

To change something, tap it, change it and tap **Save changes**. To remove it, tap it, tap **Delete**, then tap **Delete** again to be sure.

Cricket fixtures from Play-Cricket will arrive by themselves later. You will not be able to change those in the diary.

## 6. See the diary in your phone's calendar

The diary is also a Google Calendar called **Club Diary**. Subscribe once and every change appears in your phone's own calendar app. The secretary will give you a private calendar link. Do not share it outside the committee, because it shows committee only items too.

**iPhone**
1. Open **Settings**, then **Calendar**, then **Accounts**.
2. Tap **Add Account**, then **Other**, then **Add Subscribed Calendar**.
3. Paste the calendar link and tap **Next**, then **Save**.

**Android**
1. On a computer, go to calendar.google.com and sign in with the Google account your phone uses.
2. Next to **Other calendars**, click **+**, then **From URL**, and paste the calendar link.
3. Click **Add calendar**. It shows up in the Google Calendar app on your phone. If it does not, open the app, tap the menu, and tick **Club Diary**.

Subscribed calendars can be slow to update. A change can take several hours to show up in your phone's calendar. The diary page itself is always up to date, so use that if you need to be sure.

## 7. Room hire requests from the website

People ask for the **function room** or the **tea room** on the club website. The two rooms are hired separately, so one party can have the function room and another the tea room on the same evening. Nothing is booked until a committee member approves it.

When a request comes in, every committee member gets an email. It has the room, date, session and type of event, but no contact details. Open the diary to see who it is from.

**Needs approval** (on the right of the diary, or further down on a phone) lists every request waiting. Each card starts with a label saying **Function room** or **Tea room**.

* **Clash** in orange means another request, a booking, or a committee item wants the same room at the same time. Sort that out first. A request for the other room never clashes.
* **View date** shows that day in the calendar.
* **Approve** checks the room is still free and the guests fit (up to 80 in the function room, 50 in the tea room). Tea room requests for 51 to 75 guests sent before 14 November 2026 can't be approved: contact the customer to offer the function room or fewer guests (then change Guests in the Bookings tab), or decline. Approve then adds "Private party (function room)" or "Private party (tea room)" to the diary, adds a "Bar cover needed" reminder for the same session, and emails the customer to confirm, with a link to pay the £50 hire fee within 7 days. The public website then shows that session as booked for that room only. The public only ever see "Private party".
* **Decline** emails the customer to say the club can't take it. You can add a reason. It goes in the email, so keep it polite.
* **Date passed** means nobody replied before the date. Only Decline is offered, so the customer still gets an answer.
* A red **Needs attention: emails not sent** box means an email didn't go. Phone or email that customer yourself.

A request does not block the date on the website. Two people can ask for the same room on the same evening. Approve one and decline the other.

**Room bookings** lists approved bookings for both rooms still to come, with the room, the customer's phone number and email.

* **Cancel booking** asks **who is cancelling**, then emails the customer and removes the booking and its bar cover reminder from the diary.
  * **The club**: add a reason. The email says they get a full refund if they have paid.
  * **The customer asked to cancel**: put in the date they asked (it sets the refund). It can't be in the future, after the event, or before they sent their request. A note is optional. The diary shows you the refund to make, and the email tells the customer.
  * If the diary says "Please refresh the committee page and try again", your phone has an old copy of the page. Close it and open it again.
  * Then do the refund yourself in Stripe (see "Payments and refunds" below). The diary does not touch Stripe.
* You can't edit or delete an online booking from the calendar itself. Use Cancel booking so the customer is told.
* If you add an item and tick "Uses the function room" or "Uses the tea room" for a session a customer has booked in that room, the diary refuses it. Untick the box, or cancel the booking first. A morning item (finishing by 12 noon) doesn't clash with anything.

## 8. Payments and refunds

Every booking costs **£50**, whichever room and session. Nobody pays when they send a request. When you approve, the confirmation email gives the customer a Stripe payment link with their booking reference on it. They should pay within 7 days (or by the day of the event if that is sooner). The email tells them the date.

**Check who has paid** (signed in to the club's Stripe account, on the website or the Stripe app):
1. Go to **Payments**.
2. Search for the customer's **email address** (the one on the booking card).
3. Open the payment. The checkout details should show a **Client reference ID** that matches the booking reference (ECC-...).
4. Only count the booking as paid when **both** the reference **and** the email match the booking card. Anyone who has the club's payment link could pay against a made up reference, so one on its own is not enough.
5. If they match on one but not the other (for example someone paid with a different email), contact the customer and check before counting it as paid.

If someone has not paid by their date, phone or email them first. If you decide to cancel, use **Cancel booking** and choose **The club**, with a reason such as "The hire fee was not paid." They have not paid, so there is nothing to refund.

**Refunds** are done by hand in Stripe:
1. Find the payment as above.
2. Click **Refund**. Type the amount the diary showed you, then confirm.

| Who cancels | Refund if they have paid |
|---|---|
| The club (not the customer's fault) | £50, the full fee |
| Customer, 29 days or more before the event | £40 (the fee minus a £10 admin fee) |
| Customer, 14 to 28 days before | £25 |
| Customer, 13 days or fewer before | Nothing |

Days are counted from the date the customer told the club to the date of the event. For an event on Saturday 19 December, Friday 20 November is 29 days before. The amount is also written in the **Reason** column of the Bookings tab.

Stripe keeps its small fee (about 95p) on a refund. Refunds take 5 to 10 working days to reach the customer.

Bookings approved before payments started got an email with no payment link. Sort those out with the customer yourselves.

The hire terms customers agree to are at the **Hire terms link** in the Settings tab.

## Customer details

Customer details are only on this page and in the club's admin sheet. They are not in the phone calendar. They are deleted automatically 6 months after the event, or 30 days after a request is declined.

## If something goes wrong

* **"Can't reach the diary"**: check your signal or wifi and try again. Nothing was changed.
* **"That code isn't right"**: check the code with the secretary.
* **"Someone else has deleted this item"**: another committee member removed it. Close the box and carry on.
* **"The function room is no longer free"** (or "The tea room...") when you approve: something else now uses that room that session. Decline the request, or cancel the other booking first.
* **"The email couldn't be sent"**: the request or booking was saved, but the club's daily email limit was reached. Phone the customer instead.
