# Club Diary: Committee Guide

The Club Diary is where the committee and staff see what is on at the club and add or change things. It works on your phone. There is nothing to install.

## 1. Open it

Open the diary link the secretary sent you in your phone's browser (Safari on iPhone, Chrome on Android). Keep the link to the committee and staff only.

## 2. Put it on your phone's home screen

This gives you a Club Diary button, like an app.

**iPhone (Safari)**
1. Open the diary link in Safari.
2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow up).
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Tap **Add**.

**Android (Chrome)**
1. Open the diary link in Chrome.
2. Tap the three dots at the top right.
3. Tap **Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**).
4. Tap **Add**.

## 3. Sign in

1. Type your **first name**. It is only used to show who made each change.
2. Type the **diary code**. Everyone shares the same one. Ask the secretary if you do not have it.
3. Tap **Open diary**.

You stay signed in for 30 days. If the code is changed, everyone has to sign in again with the new one.

If you get the code wrong five times, the diary locks you out for 15 minutes. Wait, then try again.

## 4. Find your way around

* **Month** shows a calendar. Tap a day to see what is on it underneath. Coloured dots show there is something on that day.
* **List** shows everything coming up in the next three months, day by day.
* The coloured buttons (Club events, Cricket and so on) switch each type on or off.
* **Recent changes** at the bottom shows who changed what.
* **Sign out** is at the top.

## 5. Add an event

1. Tap **+ Add** (or **Add to this day**).
2. Type what is happening.
3. Choose the **type**, the **date**, and the **start** and **finish** times.
4. Add notes if you want. Only the committee sees notes.
5. Tick **Uses the function room** if it takes up the room. This stops the public booking it for those times.
6. Tick **Show on the public website** only if everyone should see it. Anything left unticked is for the committee only.
7. Tap **Add to diary**.

To change something, tap it, change it and tap **Save changes**. To remove it, tap it, tap **Delete**, then tap **Delete** again to be sure.

Cricket fixtures from Play-Cricket will arrive by themselves later. You will not be able to change those in the diary.

## 6. See the diary in your phone's calendar

The diary is also a Google Calendar called **Club Diary**. Subscribe once and every change appears in your phone's own calendar app. The secretary will give you a private calendar link. Do not share it outside the committee, because it shows committee only items too.

**iPhone**
1. Open **Settings**, then **Calendar**, then **Accounts**.
2. Tap **Add Account**, then **Other**, then **Add Subscribed Calendar**.
3. Paste the calendar link and tap **Next**, then **Save**.

**Android**
1. On a computer, go to calendar.google.com and sign in with the Google account your phone uses.
2. Next to **Other calendars**, click **+**, then **From URL**, and paste the calendar link.
3. Click **Add calendar**. It shows up in the Google Calendar app on your phone. If it does not, open the app, tap the menu, and tick **Club Diary**.

Subscribed calendars can be slow to update. A change can take several hours to show up in your phone's calendar. The diary page itself is always up to date, so use that if you need to be sure.

## If something goes wrong

* **"Can't reach the diary"**: check your signal or wifi and try again. Nothing was changed.
* **"That code isn't right"**: check the code with the secretary.
* **"Someone else has deleted this item"**: another committee member removed it. Close the box and carry on.
